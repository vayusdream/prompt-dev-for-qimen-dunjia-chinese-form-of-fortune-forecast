# AI Growth Agent Context

Shared language for designing the first version of the AI Growth Agent.

## Language

**AI Growth Agent**:
An AI-assisted growth system that discovers market attention, generates campaign materials, drafts human-approved launch plans, designs new-user activation paths, and monitors growth performance.

**AI Tool Product**:
A SaaS Tool Product whose core value is delivered through AI-assisted creation, analysis, automation, or decision support.
_Avoid_: generic SaaS, all software products

**AI Tool Founder**:
A founder or independent builder of an AI Tool Product who needs to connect acquisition, activation, retention, and product positioning decisions.
_Avoid_: generic entrepreneur

**Growth Lead**:
A person responsible for turning market attention into repeatable growth experiments for a SaaS Tool Product.
_Avoid_: ad operator, media buyer

**SaaS Tool Product**:
The first target business type for the AI Growth Agent. A SaaS Tool Product is evaluated through the path from acquisition to signup, first meaningful action, retention, and eventual monetization.

**Product Intelligence Loop**:
The first-version validation goal: connect campaign inputs, activation behavior, retention signals, and experience anomalies so the AI Growth Agent can recommend better growth actions over time.

**Launch Plan**:
A proposed paid or organic campaign configuration prepared by the AI Growth Agent. A Launch Plan is not live spending until a human approves it.
_Avoid_: live campaign, automatic ad buy

**Activation Path**:
The post-click journey that carries a new user from first arrival to the first meaningful product value or qualified conversion.

**Growth Experiment Package**:
The AI Growth Agent's first core activation output: a complete package containing a trend opportunity, target user hypothesis, campaign materials, launch plan, activation path, and measurement plan.
_Avoid_: report, material bundle

**Trend Opportunity**:
A market attention signal that is relevant to the target user and can be turned into a measurable Growth Experiment Package.
_Avoid_: hot topic, trend report

**Curated Source**:
A high-signal platform, feed, community, or list that the AI Growth Agent monitors in the first version.
_Avoid_: all-platform crawl

**Early Tool Community**:
The first Curated Source cluster for the AI Growth Agent, centered on Product Hunt, Hacker News, Indie Hackers, and Reddit communities where AI Tool Products are discovered and discussed.
_Avoid_: general social media

**Submitted Signal**:
A trend clue supplied by the user, such as a link, keyword, screenshot, post, competitor example, or topic description.
_Avoid_: manual workaround

**Experience Anomaly**:
A point in the Activation Path where acquisition, activation, retention, or behavior metrics suggest user friction, broken expectations, or measurement gaps.
